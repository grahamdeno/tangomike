The apps

Five iPhone apps, each with its own home. Follow any of them to learn more or join the beta.

Scales

On the App Store

One thought at a time.

Write down the thoughts you want to live by. Scales brings them back one at a time so you can sit with them, argue with them, and put away the ones you now live.

scales.tangomike.app · Get it on the App Store

Shoebox

Beta

Keep it. Come back to it.

Links, screenshots and notes you meant to get back to come to you as your own calm feed, a few a day. Open it, save it for later, or let it go.

inmyshoebox.com

Pop In

On the App Store

Lose track of time, not calls.

Pop In rings a real alarm before every meeting on your iPhone calendar. Your next meeting is a gum bubble that grows as it gets closer; pop it to join. It only reads your calendar, and nothing leaves your phone.

popin.tangomike.app

Held Dear

Coming in December

Every day that matters, done with care.

Held Dear keeps each birthday, anniversary and holiday for the people you love on one line through your year, and helps you plan the whole thing: the card, the call, the gift and the cake. No account and no server; it all stays on your iPhone and in your own iCloud.

helddear.tangomike.app

Rep Bell

Coming soon

Every set rings.

Rep Bell runs the 14-day push-up program on iPhone and Apple Watch. It works out every set from your max, rings when each one is due, and logs it in one tap. Workouts and a coach voice that reads your numbers are a one-time unlock. No account; everything stays on your devices.

repbell.tangomike.app

How they're built

One thing at a time

Your things stay close

Where it can, your data and the AI that reads it stay on your iPhone.

Easy to put down

No streaks and no endless scroll. When you're done, the app lets you go.
